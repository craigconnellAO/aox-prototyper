# Discovery

Living record of research, evidence, assumptions, and learnings for the Switch24 mobile purchasing experience.

Switch24 is AO's mobile proposition that combines handset financing, membership benefits, and connected mobile services into a simpler, more customer-friendly buying journey.

This document captures why we're building Switch24, what we've learned so far, and what evidence should drive future design decisions.

---

## Project Context

Buying a mobile phone online remains unnecessarily complex.

Customers are often asked to navigate multiple products, separate brands, confusing pricing structures, disconnected journeys, and aggressive upsell patterns before they can complete a purchase.

The ambition behind Switch24 is to simplify that experience.

Instead of treating phone finance, connectivity, membership benefits, trade-in, insurance, and future services as separate products, Switch24 aims to present them as part of a coherent and transparent mobile ownership experience.

Success means customers can:

- Understand exactly what they're getting
- Understand exactly what they're paying
- Complete their journey quickly
- Feel confident in their decision
- Stay within a single trusted AO experience

---

## Problem Statement

Current pay-monthly journeys across the market suffer from a number of recurring issues:

### Customer Problems

- Pricing is difficult to understand
- Handset and plan costs are often separated
- Add-ons appear unexpectedly throughout the journey
- Customers feel pressured by upsell mechanics
- Journeys become fragmented across multiple screens and systems
- It is difficult to compare options confidently

### Business Problems

- High abandonment rates
- Low attachment rates for value-added services
- Increased support burden caused by confusion
- Reduced customer trust
- Missed opportunities to deepen customer relationships

---

## Research Themes

### Pricing Transparency

Across both competitor analysis and user research, transparent pricing repeatedly emerges as one of the strongest drivers of trust.

Customers want:

- Total monthly cost visible early
- Costs updated in real time
- No hidden fees
- Clear explanation of what contributes to the monthly payment

**Implication**

Switch24 should surface total monthly cost as a primary element throughout the journey rather than a final checkout calculation.

---

### Phone First, Everything Else Second

Customers begin with a device decision.

Features such as:

- Data allowance
- Finance options
- Trade-in
- Insurance
- Membership benefits

are considered only after the customer has confidence in their handset choice.

**Implication**

The handset remains the anchor of the experience.

Supporting decisions should feel like natural extensions rather than separate purchasing journeys.

---

### Low Tolerance For Complexity

Research consistently shows:

- Customers want shorter journeys
- Customers prefer fewer screens
- Customers dislike repeated decisions
- Customers become frustrated by unnecessary steps

Common abandonment triggers include:

- Too many pages
- Re-entering information
- Unclear progress
- Excessive upselling

**Implication**

Every step introduced into Switch24 must justify its existence.

Complexity should be removed wherever possible.

---

### Additives Should Feel Optional

Trade-in, insurance, accessories, and future services can add value.

However customers react negatively when they feel forced into those decisions too early.

**Implication**

Optional services should appear as contextual enhancements rather than mandatory steps.

---

## Key UX Hypotheses

### H1

Showing a live total monthly price throughout configuration will improve confidence and reduce abandonment.

### H2

Combining core ownership decisions onto a single configuration experience will outperform a traditional multi-step funnel.

### H3

Presenting data-plan selection immediately after device selection aligns with customer mental models.

### H4

Reducing perceived complexity will improve completion rate more than increasing promotional messaging.

### H5

Customers will respond more positively to recommendation-based guidance than comparison-heavy telco-style interfaces.

---

## Design Principles

### Price Confidence

Customers should never need to calculate their own payment.

### One Journey

Switch24 should feel like a single coherent experience.

### Configure, Don't Navigate

Where possible, customers should adjust choices within a view rather than moving between pages.

### Progressive Disclosure

Reveal information when needed rather than upfront.

### Optional Means Optional

Customers should never feel trapped in an upsell flow.

### AO Trust

Switch24 benefits from AO's existing brand trust and should reinforce that relationship rather than compete with it.
