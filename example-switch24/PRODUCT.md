# Product

## Product

Switch24

---

## Users

Primary users are customers purchasing a mobile phone through AO.

They may be:

- Replacing an existing device
- Switching provider
- Upgrading their contract
- Moving from SIM-only to a new handset
- Seeking a more affordable monthly payment option

Most customers have moderate purchase intent and limited patience for complexity.

They want confidence, value, and simplicity.

---

## Product Purpose

Switch24 exists to make mobile purchasing easier.

Rather than forcing customers through separate handset, finance, connectivity, trade-in, membership, and protection journeys, Switch24 brings those decisions together into a single coherent experience.

The goal is not simply to sell a phone.

The goal is to help customers leave AO with everything they need to get started while feeling fully confident about what they've chosen.

---

## Vision

Create the simplest way to buy and own a mobile phone online.

Customers should be able to:

- Choose a phone
- Understand their monthly cost
- Configure their ownership package
- Complete checkout

without confusion, friction, or unnecessary decision-making.

---

## Commercial Goals

### Increase Conversion

Reduce abandonment throughout the mobile purchase journey.

### Improve Finance Adoption

Increase uptake of Switch24 finance products.

### Increase Service Attachment

Create natural opportunities for:

- Mobile plans
- Membership
- Protection products
- Future subscription services

### Increase Customer Lifetime Value

Build longer-term customer relationships beyond a single device purchase.

### Reduce Support Costs

Improve clarity and understanding throughout the experience.

## Brand Personality

### Confident

Clear, decisive, trustworthy.

### Helpful

Explains complex topics in simple language.

### Straightforward

No jargon. No industry theatre.

### Optimistic

Positive and encouraging without becoming sales-driven.

---

## What Switch24 Is Not

### Not A Telco

Avoid:

- Coverage maps as hero content
- Speed-stat marketing
- Technical jargon
- Feature overload

### Not A Deal Site

Avoid:

- Excessive urgency
- Countdown timers
- Artificial scarcity
- Aggressive promotional mechanics

### Not A Sales Funnel

Avoid:

- Endless step progression
- Repeated upsell interruptions
- Hidden costs
- Forced decisions

---

## Design Principles

### AO Trust, Switch24 Simplicity

Leverage AO familiarity while simplifying the mobile category.

### Show The Cost

Pricing should remain visible throughout key decision moments.

### Minimise Cognitive Load

Reduce unnecessary decisions wherever possible.

### Guide, Don't Sell

Help customers choose rather than push customers toward choices.

### Extend Naturally

Additional services should feel like useful enhancements rather than separate purchases.

### Consistency Matters

Use AO design system components, patterns, tokens, and accessibility standards throughout.

---

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Switch24 must meet AO accessibility standards and WCAG 2.1 AA requirements.

Requirements include:

- Keyboard accessibility
- Screen-reader compatibility
- Clear focus states
- Sufficient colour contrast
- Mobile-first responsive behaviour
- Accessible form patterns
- Non-colour-dependent feedback

---

## Design System

The AO Design System is the source of truth.

Switch24 should:

- Use approved tokens
- Use approved components
- Follow existing interaction patterns
- Avoid introducing custom visual systems unnecessarily

The product's character should come primarily from:

- Information hierarchy
- Layout
- Content strategy
- Tone of voice

rather than from bespoke visual styling.

---

## Definition of Success

A successful Switch24 experience allows customers to:

- Understand their options quickly
- Configure their purchase confidently
- Complete checkout efficiently
- Feel positive about their decision

while helping AO achieve stronger conversion, attachment, and long-term customer value.
